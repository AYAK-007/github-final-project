# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## Expected Behavior
- Treat everyone with respect and kindness.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback respectfully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Threats, intimidation, or abusive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer using
the contact information on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive
information in public issues.

## Enforcement
The project maintainer will review reports fairly and confidentially.
Violations may result in a warning, removal of contributions, or a
temporary or permanent ban from participation.

## Scope
This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other community spaces.
